A little atmosphere. On your desktop.

Snow settles on your windows. Rain splashes into reflective puddles. Mist curls around your cursor, and fireflies leave glowing trails. Choose a scene, or let Live Weather follow the sky outside.

On your windows

It settles where it lands.

Snow gathers on the tops of your windows and the Dock, following their rounded corners. Drag a window and its snow slides off; close or minimize it and the snow falls to the drift below.

Snowfall

A small snow demo — Aeolian has twelve scenes and Live Weather. This menu works. Try Heavy, turn off Melt Snow and watch the drift grow, or flip Show in Screenshots and see what a screenshot keeps.

Weather that moves with you

Follows the sky
Live Weather blends scenes to match local conditions. Choose Apple Weather or Open-Meteo, with the location, conditions and active source clearly shown.
Settles
It piles up and slumps to a natural slope along the bottom of the screen, on any window with room above it, and on the Dock.
Reacts
Sweep snow and leaves aside, stir volumetric mist, or throw water into splashes. Drag a window and its puddles carry the momentum.
Reflects
Rain gathers into interactive water on windows, the Dock and the ground. Reflect your live desktop, cached window snapshots or just the wallpaper.
Changes naturally
Rain arrives while the previous snow melts. Mist disperses and puddles dry as the next scene takes over.
Hides
Screenshots and screen sharing show your desktop without it, unless you turn on Show in Screenshots.
Rests
Animation follows your display’s native refresh rate, including ProMotion. Aeolian pauses while your screen sleeps, locks or is covered by a full-screen app.

Privacy

Your desktop stays yours.

Last updated 9 November 2026. Aeolian is developed by Scott Graeme Robb. Questions: support@grobb.info.

Location and local weather

Location access is optional. When you enable Live Weather, Aeolian requests your location from macOS. Weather requests send coordinates rounded to approximately one kilometre to Apple Weather, or to Open-Meteo when selected or used as the labelled fallback. Apple reverse geocoding uses coordinates rounded to approximately 100 metres to display a neighbourhood name. Providers receive the network information needed to service a request, such as your IP address. Open-Meteo states that its troubleshooting logs may include IP addresses and the rounded coordinates and are deleted after 90 days. Requests are subject to Apple’s privacy policy and Open-Meteo’s terms and privacy information.

Aeolian does not operate a location database or store a location history. Location and weather readings are held in memory. You can turn Live Weather off or revoke Location Services access in System Settings. Manual atmospheres work without location.

Optional desktop screenshots

Screenshot Access enables occasional reduced-resolution window and desktop images for water reflections and fog bloom. Images are processed in memory on your Mac, are never uploaded, and are not saved as recordings or screenshots. Aeolian does not record video, audio or microphone input. Capture stops when visible reflections or bloom no longer need it, and while the display is asleep or locked. Wallpaper-only reflections work without screenshot access. macOS manages screenshot permissions and access indicators.

Settings and updates

Presets, appearance choices and preferences are stored locally on your Mac. Optional personal WeatherKit credentials are stored in macOS Keychain. Direct-download versions contact GitHub to check and download releases; GitHub receives normal network request information under its privacy policy. App Store versions must use Apple’s update system.

No advertising or accounts

Aeolian has no accounts, adverts, subscriptions, purchases or advertising trackers. It does not upload desktop contents or send product analytics to the developer. If you contact support, the information you choose to send is used to respond to your request. Uninstalling Aeolian removes the application; permissions can be revoked in System Settings and saved settings can be removed from your Mac separately.
